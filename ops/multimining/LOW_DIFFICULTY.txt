LOW-DIFFICULTY PARENT TARGETS

Each share target must cover the easier of the child and parent network targets.
Otherwise a miner discards hashes that are valid blocks on one of the chains.
The bridge caps share difficulty conservatively, including fractional difficulty,
before storing a job. This applies to initial jobs, refreshes and vardiff updates.
Per-job difficulty is preserved for late submissions.

Tests cover both parent/child target orders. The correction is chain-independent;
the configured parent remains Kaspa. No node consensus changes are needed.
